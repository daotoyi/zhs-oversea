Origins · 道之源

From a silk manuscript
found in a tomb.

In 1973, workers excavating a tomb in Changsha opened a sealed silk scroll that had been untouched for two thousand years. Eighty-one chapters. No rhyme, no polish — the raw text.

Our line of study begins there. Every chapter we teach is read from that manuscript rather than the later, smoother editions the West has read for two centuries.

Every chapter of the Dao De Jing is a prompt, not a lecture. We pair the text with a weekly practice and a set of objects that make it easier to keep.

Chapter 12

The Way of the Sage in the World

道 法 自 然
“The sage manages affairs without doing. He acts without assuming credit for his deeds. He is content with the way things are, without ever being attached to it.”

Note the translation is deliberately awkward in places. That is the silk text's own rhythm: it was written to be read aloud, two thousand years before anyone decided Daoism should sound elegant.

Traceability

How to know
what you're holding.

Most sellers of Daoist objects online will not tell you where the material came from, what the metal contains, or who consecrated it. We publish it on every product page — because this is how you tell handwork from manufacturing.

  • Workshop and maker named, with region of origin
  • Material declared in full — species, alloy, finish
  • ISO 17025 laboratory report number, viewable
  • CITES appendix status stated plainly on wooden and resin pieces
  • Consecration certificate number, validatable by lineage

Each ritual object carries a certificate number issued by the lineage that prepared it, and the name of the practitioner. The certificate describes a specific rite and a specific date. Objects ship from Beijing, insured, with tracking. US delivery runs 7–15 days; UK, Canada and EU 9–18 days. Shipping is free on US orders over $60. Orders under $60 pay a flat $12 at checkout, so the price you see is the price you pay — duties and taxes are settled at checkout, never collected on delivery.
Natural cinnabar contains mercury sulfide, and the EU restricts mercury in jewellery to zero. We therefore do not sell cinnabar as something to wear against the skin. Our cinnabar pieces are sealed inside a silver cage or mounted for display, and each ships with an ISO 17025 heavy-metal assay report you can read before buying. If you want a piece for daily wear, we will recommend an alternative instead.
Some species — agarwood, sandalwood, rosewood — are listed under CITES Appendix II, including when plantation-grown, so we can only ship them with the export certificate that accompanies the material. Every wooden product page states its CITES status before you order. If a piece cannot be certified, it is not listed.
